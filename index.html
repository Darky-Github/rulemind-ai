How RuleMind transforms user input before intent detection.

Basic Normalization
Converts text to lowercase, removes unnecessary punctuation, and normalizes whitespace.

Converts abbreviations such as r, u, thx, pls, and idk into their full forms.
Alias Expansion
Maps alternative words and phrases to canonical vocabulary.
Typo Correction
Corrects known spelling mistakes and uses fuzzy matching for unknown minor mistakes.
Repeated Letters
Handles inputs such as heyyy, goooood, and howww.
Final Tokenization
Produces normalized tokens used by the intent matching system.
